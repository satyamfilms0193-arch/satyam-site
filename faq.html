Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking us

1. How far in advance should we book you?

We recommend booking 6–12 months in advance, especially for wedding seasons, to secure your preferred dates.

2. Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes! We cover weddings across India and internationally. Travel and accommodation charges are additional.

3. What is included in your wedding packages?

Our packages include photography, videography, edited photos, highlight films, and full-length cinematic videos. Drone and premium albums are part of higher packages.

4. Do you provide raw images and videos?

We provide all high-resolution edited images and final films. Raw data can be shared upon special request.

5. How long does it take to deliver the final album and videos?

Highlight films are delivered within 2-3 weeks, while complete albums and cinematic films are delivered within 6-8 weeks.

6. Do you offer pre-wedding shoots?

Absolutely! Pre-wedding shoots are one of our specialties, and we help select locations, outfits, and themes.

7. How do we confirm our booking?

Bookings are confirmed upon signing an agreement and making an advance payment (usually 30% of the package cost).

8. Can we customize packages?

Yes, all packages are customizable to suit your wedding needs, budget, and creative preferences.

9. What happens if the wedding date changes?

We will do our best to accommodate the new date if available. In case of cancellation, our cancellation policy will apply.

10. Do you provide same-day edit videos?

Yes! Same-day edits can be arranged with prior notice for an additional cost, perfect for wedding receptions.
